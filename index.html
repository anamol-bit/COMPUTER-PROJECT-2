Anamol Shrestha

Child Rights Advocate | Student | Future Prime Minister

About Me

My name is Anamol Shrestha. I am a child rights advocate and I love doing social service. I believe every child deserves a safe and happy childhood.

Helping others is not just what I do — it's who I am. I work with local communities to raise awareness about child protection and education.

Education

I am currently studying at St. Xavier College, Maitighar. I have graduated my class 10 from Bal Geetanjali School. I am in 12th grade and my favorite subject is Literature.

I also enjoy helping my juniors with their studies.

My Interests & Hobbies

Things I Love

Nature Walks

I love walking in nature and spending time outdoors. Walking in the woods or by the river helps me stay calm and think clearly.

Newari Culture

I like to explore Newari culture — the food, festivals, and traditional architecture. It feels like connecting with my roots. Newa: Jwajalapa!

Political Debates

I enjoy political debates because they sharpen my thinking and help me understand different perspectives. I often debate with my friends about policy and governance.

Besides these, I also like reading literature and writing poems in my free time.

My Future Goal

"My dream is to become the Prime Minister of Nepal and work for the rights and well-being of every child in the country."

I want to create a Nepal where no child is left behind. Education, healthcare, and safety should be basic rights, not privileges.

— Anamol Shrestha
